Deaf Accessibility in South African Healthcare 🇿🇦

About the Project

DeafHealthSA is a project focused on improving healthcare accessibility for Deaf individuals in South Africa.

The project explores communication barriers experienced by Deaf patients when accessing healthcare services and highlights the importance of inclusive communication, South African Sign Language (SASL), and accessible healthcare solutions.

Objectives

Identify communication barriers faced by Deaf patients.

Explore healthcare accessibility challenges in South Africa.

Promote awareness of South African Sign Language (SASL).

Use Power BI to analyse and present relevant healthcare accessibility data.

Support more inclusive healthcare services for Deaf individuals.
